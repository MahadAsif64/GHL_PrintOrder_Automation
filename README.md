**Print Order Automation System** is a fully automated, end-to-end print order management solution built on GoHighLevel (GHL) for a print shop serving the National League of Cities, Gastonia, NC. Designed to eliminate manual processing entirely, the system handles two core print products — Tri-Fold brochures and Parents' Reception Cards — through a seamlessly orchestrated digital workflow.

**Order Intake**
The process begins with a smart, multi-step intake form featuring conditional logic that dynamically adapts based on the customer's selections. This ensures only relevant fields are presented, reducing errors and improving the ordering experience for both customers and staff.

**Proof Generation & Approval**
Once an order is submitted, the system automatically generates a professional PDF proof tailored to the customer's specifications. The proof is delivered digitally for review, and e-signature collection is handled automatically — eliminating back-and-forth emails and phone calls that traditionally slow down the approval process.

**Invoicing & Payments**
Upon proof approval, the system triggers automatic Stripe invoice creation with the correct pricing, quantities, and order details pre-populated. Customers receive a professional invoice directly and can complete payment without any manual intervention from the admin or directors.

**Shipping & Tracking**
After payment confirmation, the system dispatches automated shipping notifications integrated with USPS tracking, keeping customers informed of their order status from dispatch through to delivery — without anyone on the team lifting a finger.

**Workflow Automation**
At the heart of the system are seven published GHL workflows that connect every stage of the process. These workflows handle triggers, conditional branching, notifications, document generation, signature requests, invoice creation, and shipping updates — forming a complete, self-running operational pipeline.

**Key Highlights**
- Smart conditional intake forms for accurate order capture
- Automated PDF proof generation and delivery
- Digital e-signature collection without manual follow-up
- Stripe-integrated automatic invoice creation
- USPS tracking with automated shipping notifications
- Seven GHL workflows covering the full order lifecycle
- Zero manual processing steps for admins and directors

The result is a fast, error-free, and fully hands-off print order operation that saves significant time and delivers a professional experience to every customer.
